## 🌟 Concept & Overview

**Everything Upgrade Tree** is an expansive incremental universe inspired by the legendary *Everything Upgrade Tree (EUT)*. Players begin with a solitary, pulsing point of genesis—the **Primordial Singularity**—situated in an infinite void of dark space.

By channeling manual kinetic energy into the core, players harvest **Raw Matter (₽)**, slowly revealing a breathtaking constellation of automated harvesters, synergistic resonance nodes, epistemological research centers, and cascading multi-tier reset dimensions.

---

## 🌌 Core Progression & Reset Layers

1. **Baseplate 1: Points (₽) & Research (λ)**
   - Manual clicking of the central Singularity with critical burst multipliers.
   - 6 tiers of automated harvesters (Quantum Harvester, Tachyon Siphon, Antimatter Sieve, Chrono Dynamo, Dyson Swarmlet, Vacuum Synthesizer).
   - Compound percentage multipliers (Harmonic Resonance, Fractal Arrays, Entanglement Coils, Hyper-Density Weaver).
   - **Lambda Research Lab:** Synthesize theoretical insights (λ) that log-boost matter production and compress upgrade costs across the universe.

2. **Baseplate 2: The Great Prestige Conduit (₹)**
   - **The First Major Paradigm Shift:** Collapse your entire matter and automation empire to crystallize permanent **Prestige Points (₹)**.
   - Unlock persistent automation retention (*Eternal Genesis*), exponential prestige multipliers (*Astral Catalyst*), an automated AI upgrade governor (*Autonomous Overseer*), and passive prestige feedback.

3. **Baseplate 3: Transcension Threshold (τ)**
   - **The Cosmic Ascension:** Ascend past baryonic matter to earn **Transcendence Points (τ)**.
   - Powers astronomical mechanics, galactic architecture, and the Event Horizon.

## 🎛 Deep Unlockable Mechanics

- **⛏️ Subterranean Extraction Quarry (Mining):**
  - Interactive 6x6 quarry of destructible blocks.
  - Pickaxe tiers (Stone, Copper, Iron, Ruby Laser, Singularity Smasher).
  - Alloy Forge: smelt Copper Wiring (+25% automation), Iron Plating (-15% costs), Ruby Infusion (+100% production), and Celestial Alloy (+200% prestige).
- **🃏 Arcane Deck Archive (Cards & Decks):**
  - Buy packs: *Static Pack*, *Foodaholic Pack*, *Cosmic Astral Pack*.
  - Rarity tiers: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Astral (with animated holographic foil).
  - 3 Equippable Deck Slots granting massive active multipliers.
- **🔭 Stellar Observatory & Supermassive Black Hole:**
  - Purchase real constellation stars (Alpha Centauri, Sirius B, Betelgeuse, Polaris).
  - Feed raw matter to the Supermassive Black Hole to expand its Schwarzchild radius and bend space with gravitational lensing.
- **🧩 Quantum Logic Terminal (Minesweeper):**
  - Fully functional 8x8 Minesweeper logic board.
  - Revealing safe cells grants instant Matter rushes.
  - Clearing grids awards permanent **Quantum Relics** (+50% global production each).
- **⚔️ Trials of the Void (Challenges):**
  - *Zero-Automation Trial*, *Entropy Deceleration*, and *Singularity Collapse*.
  - Endure severe reality handicaps to claim permanent meta-perks.

## 🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

- **Mouse:** Left-click to click nodes or mine; Click & drag anywhere on the void to pan; Mouse wheel to zoom in/out.
- **Minimap:** Click anywhere on the bottom-right radar to jump coordinates instantly.
- **Keyboard:**
  - `Space`: Manual Click Singularity
  - `W / A / S / D` or Arrows: Pan canvas
  - `C`: Center camera on Origin
  - `SFX / Drone`: Audio toggles in top bar

## 🚀 Quick Start

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/arezoyetoo-dotcom/everything-upgrade-tree.git
cd everything-upgrade-tree

# Install express server
npm install

# Start local server on port 5407
npm start
# or double-click launchers/games/everything-upgrade-tree.html in Windows!
```
